========================================
  MEECHYLAND - HOW TO PLAY (for Mila!)
========================================

Hi Mila! This is your own special Minecraft that works in the internet browser.

You get GOD mode (you can fly, never die, and build anything).

Grown-up help is needed for the first setup. After that you can play by yourself!

----------------------------------------
STEP 1 - Ask a grown-up to start the server
----------------------------------------
The grown-up follows the file called:
  GROWN_UP_SERVER_SETUP.txt

----------------------------------------
STEP 2 - Open MeechyLand in your browser
----------------------------------------
1. Open Chrome or Edge
2. Go to the special link the grown-up gives you
   (or open the file meechyland-index.html if it is on your computer)

3. Type your name: Mila
4. Click Multiplayer
5. Click "MeechyLand Family Server"
6. Click Join!

----------------------------------------
STEP 3 - You are in GOD mode!
----------------------------------------
- Press space twice to fly
- Press space to go up
- Press shift to go down
- You cannot die
- You have a special starter kit in your inventory

----------------------------------------
YOUR SPECIAL STARTER ITEMS
----------------------------------------
When you join, a grown-up can give you these (or they auto-give if set up):

- Mila's Magic Sword (super strong)
- Mila's Flying Boots
- Rainbow Blocks
- Sparkle Blocks
- Mila's Building Kit
- Infinite food and tools

Ask the grown-up to run the commands in:
  GIVE_MILA_HER_STUFF.txt

----------------------------------------
FUN THINGS TO DO
----------------------------------------
- Build a giant castle
- Make a rainbow house
- Fly around the whole world
- Invite family to join

Have fun in MeechyLand!
